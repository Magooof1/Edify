## Retirement Planning University — Facebook ad, Xavier University, October 2026

`2026-10-rpu-xavier-fb-ad-1.png`, `-2.png`. Screenshots from Justin's own feed, supplied 2026-10-09
("I found this online"). Sponsored post.

- **Event:** Xavier University, Cincinnati. **Wednesday 14 and Thursday 15 October 2026, 6:00 to 8:30 pm**
  (two consecutive evenings; whether it is one class in two parts or the same class twice is not stated).
- **Hook:** one topic, taxes. Image headline *"The IRS is your silent partner in retirement. Have you
  negotiated?"*; watercolour illustration of a retiree on a beach sharing his drink with a hooded "IRS"
  figure; yellow brush-stroke date band. Link headline *"Will Taxes Impact Your Retirement?"*
- **Body:** "The IRS may not retire when you do. Taxes can still affect Social Security, retirement account
  withdrawals, investment income, and the money you keep." Then: "Free Retirement Planning Workbook
  Included", **"No high-pressure sales presentation"**, "Seats are limited. Reserve your spot before this
  class fills."

**What it adds to the record (Isla, 2026-10-09):**
- RPU is active on **both** sides of the river: NKU Tuesday 6 pm sessions (Eventbrite listings, 2026) and now
  Xavier. Both are universities. It is buying Facebook reach, not only listings.
- Its reassurance line is **"no high-pressure sales presentation"**, which concedes a presentation. Our
  registration panel says **"Nothing is sold in the room and there's nothing to sign"**, a stronger claim
  and a real point of difference, **but it is true only once the no-pitch note is signed.**
- It uses scarcity, which Justin has ruled out for us. It offers a physical takeaway (a workbook).
- **Weakest reading:** one ad, one screenshot. It shows the category is crowded at university venues in
  autumn 2026; it does not show RPU is mailing NKU's ZIPs in January. The mailbox log is what would.
