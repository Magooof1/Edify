## The architecture, which the marketing work had wrong

**The Financial House is the master frame. The three buckets sit inside it.**

- **Roof** — health and long-term care
- **Walls** — income (plan) and legacy
- **Foundation** — money (protect)
- **YOU** at the centre

Three Buckets appears partway through Part 2, *inside the money foundation*, after
market cycles and the 4% rule have set it up. Every marketing piece built so far leads
with the buckets instead.

That isn't automatically wrong — the buckets are what converts, per the eval-form data
in `.claude/memory/growth-channels.md` — but the gap between how Justin teaches it and
how we've been selling it has never been examined. Justin's whiteboard photo
(`reference/brand-assets/justin-whiteboard-three-buckets.jpg`) shows both together: the
house with YOU inside, three circles beneath.

## Assets in here that no marketing piece has used

- **Fiduciary vs. suitability** (Part 1) — a factual distinction, compliance-safe, and
  a differentiator most advisors won't put on a slide.
- **"What's My Gap?"** (Part 2) — a worked income-shortfall example with real numbers.
  Structurally the same device as the mirror: the reader reaches their own conclusion.
- **Protected vs. non-protected income** (Part 2) — the income-side analogue of the
  bucket frame.
- **Sourcing** — Boston College CRR, Society of Actuaries, J.P. Morgan's Guide to
  Retirement, DALBAR, BLS. Citable, and already vetted.
