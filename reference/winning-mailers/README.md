# Winning mailers — the control

Pieces that actually worked in Justin's market. **Read the control before designing a
challenger.** This folder exists because for two days this workforce designed mail
against a standard nobody had ever looked at, and produced something Justin rejected on
sight.

In direct response, the "control" is the piece that currently pulls best. You don't
replace it because you have a better idea. You try to beat it, and you find out.

## 2021 Spring Learning Program — NKU

`2021-spring-nku-seminar.pdf` (text extracted alongside for grepping)

11×17 folded self-mailer. Printed March 2021, mailed for April 2021 classes. Presort
standard, Louisville KY permit — High Note's imprint.

**Result: this one produced $6M.** Justin, 2026-09-19.

Three sessions offered: "Planning And Coordinating Your Retirement" on April 10 and
April 17, and "Financial Empowerment for Women" on April 23. All Saturday 8:30am–12:30pm
at NKU's Student Union Building.

**Justin's own read, in his words:** nobody signed up for the women's session. It was
also a deliberate test — "multiple classes makes it look more like a learning
environment." Note that a session drawing zero attendees is not evidence the test
failed. The multi-class structure is part of what makes the piece read as a course
catalog rather than a seminar invitation, and that framing may be load-bearing for the
whole piece. Don't drop it on the strength of one empty session without knowing which
job it was doing.

## Caveats before copying anything from it

- **"Due to health/safety guidelines, seating is limited"** appears on the registration
  panel. That is scarcity framing, and Ed's 2026-09-12 hard wall bars it. It passed in
  2021 because COVID made it a statement of fact. It does not transfer.
- **The disclosure block is 2021-vintage and conflicts with the current record** on
  which entity sells insurance and annuities. Flagged to Justin 2026-09-19; unresolved
  as of this writing. Do not take entity facts from this piece — the standing rule is
  that entity facts come from Justin directly, never inferred from a document, and this
  is exactly the document that would mislead you.
- The piece states "ages of 50 and 70." Justin currently describes his mail target as
  50-65.
