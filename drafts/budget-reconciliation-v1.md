# Budget reconciliation — one plan, not three (Isla, 2026-10-01)

**Written after Justin confirmed three facts that none of the three directors had when
they planned.** Their revisions each claimed nearly the whole pot, so this is an
allocation decision rather than a merge. **Isla's call, and the disagreements are named.**

## The three facts that changed the arithmetic

1. **The constraint, confirmed:** channel, media and service spend is in — any channel,
   FB ads, mailers, workshops, rooms, client appreciation, an editor's labour. **Capital
   equipment is out. Website design is out. Digital lead-gen is fine. Justin builds the
   landing page himself**, so it costs $0 rather than being excluded.
2. **The spring mailer sits INSIDE the pot.** The 2026 spring mailer is already spent,
   leaving **$7,000 for 2026**. The **2027 $22,000 includes next spring's mailer.**
3. **The book is $22M at about 1%** — roughly **$220,000/year** recurring.

could not.**

## And it is decidable for $1,500 of money that expires anyway

**Sloan's own arithmetic argues against her own retainer.** An editor buys back a
Justin-hour at about **$275** against a Justin-hour worth about **$110**. It is a
*completion* argument, not an hour-value argument — it pays only if **editing**, rather
than **shooting**, is what stops the course existing. Her irreducible cost is ~2.5 hours
per video **even with an editor.**

**Her Q4 batch day settles it empirically.** Four chapters in one day. **Video 1 unaided
with hours logged. Videos 2–4 edited, paid from the expiring 2026 money.** Justin's own
logged hours then show what an editor buys, before a single 2027 dollar commits.

**This is the only line in any plan that can start this week** — shooting and editing need
no compliance clearance. Only *publishing* needs Ed, which is the correct sequencing under
the 2026-10-01 rule. **It needs a date before 15 November.**
