## 2. What happens at the first visit

- The agenda is driven by the attendee's own stated areas of concern, not by Justin's
  selection. For a class attendee this is the checkbox section of the Step 2 Form —
  Foundation (Investments), Income, Legacy, Health & Care — checked by the attendee
  before the visit is scheduled. [BLANK — for a visit booked directly from mail or web

  something else? Needed so the "attendee sets the agenda" claim is true for both entry
  points, not just the class-connected one.]
- Justin discusses the checked/stated areas of concern in conversation. Per the script,
  this includes questions such as: how much Social Security the attendee has and when to
  take it, how a pension coordinates, what monthly expense need exists and whether
  inflation-adjusted, and whether there are gaps between income and expenses.
- If the attendee has brought documents (see Section 4 below), Justin reviews them in
  the visit to answer the question "what do you already have" — see Section 4 for why
  this is diagnostic rather than a sale.

## 3. What does not happen at the first visit

Stated here as policy, not aspiration:

- **No specific product is recommended.** No insurance product, annuity, or investment
  product is proposed or discussed as something the attendee should purchase.
- **No application is taken and nothing is signed.** The first visit does not include
  paperwork of any kind that commits the attendee to anything.
- **No solicitation.** The visit is not used to solicit the sale of an insurance or
  investment product.

## 4. What happens when an attendee brings documents

The class script and the mailer both tell the attendee to *"bring whatever's most
comfortable for you"* — including nothing. When an attendee does bring account
statements or other documents to a first visit, the following applies as policy:

- Documents are reviewed **only** to answer the diagnostic question of what the
  attendee currently holds and how it maps to the three categories Justin uses
  (Principal Protected / Manage Risk — Tactical Investments / Accept Risk — Buy and
  Hold Investments, per the Step 2 Form's own labels).
- This review is **not** a recommendation, proposal, or sales presentation. Identifying
  what an attendee already owns is not the same act as recommending what they should
  own, and this policy treats the two as categorically separate: the first happens at a
  first visit, the second does not.
- No product is proposed based on what the documents show, at the first visit.
