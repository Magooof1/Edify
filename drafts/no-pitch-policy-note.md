## 5. At the visit: what is done and what is not

**Done.** Justin discusses the attendee's areas of concern. He tells the attendee, in his
words from the close, *"how much green, how much blue, and how much red you have."* Without
documents that is a conversation and a rough read from what the attendee describes. With
documents it is more precise. *"Bring whatever's most comfortable for you. The more you bring,
the more precise we can be."* Documents are reviewed only to answer what the attendee
currently holds and how it maps to the three categories.

**Not done at this visit.**

- No specific product is recommended or proposed as something to buy.
- No application is taken. Nothing is signed.
- If a product conversation makes sense, it happens in a separate, later meeting, which the
  attendee has been told is coming (Justin's decision, 2026-09-12).

**Consequence for the wording of pages and pieces.** A read of someone's actual holdings is
individualized. The visit is therefore **not** described as "not individualized advice" on
the registration page, the confirmation email or the mailer. "Not individualized advice" stays
on the class, which is general education. The Block A disclosure carries the rest.

**Documents.** Statements an attendee brings are used for this review and then [per block
