## FRONT

**Two Minutes. Five Questions. Your Own Money, In Your Own Words.**

You don't need to know anything about investing to answer these. You just need to
know your own accounts. There's no right answer to any of them — just yours.

Answer yes or no.

1. Do you have money in a savings account, a CD, or an annuity that's set up so the
   original amount you put in can't go down?
   ☐ Yes ☐ No

2. Could you get to some of that money within a few days if you needed it — without
   a penalty, and without asking anyone's permission?
   ☐ Yes ☐ No

3. Do you have a 401(k), 403(b), or an IRA left over from a job you don't work at
   anymore?
   ☐ Yes ☐ No

4. The last time the market dropped sharply — 2022, 2020, 2008, whichever you
   remember — did any of your account balances drop right along with it, with
   nothing in place to limit how far they fell?
   ☐ Yes ☐ No

5. Right now, could you name one part of your retirement savings that's invested
   for growth but has a specific plan in place limiting how much it could lose in
   a bad year?
   ☐ Yes ☐ No

*Turn over to see what your answers show you.*

## Notes for the record (not for print)

1. **Bucket names deliberately not used.** Green/Blue/Red stay in-class. This card
   never uses them — see reasoning in the accompanying report to Isla/Justin.
2. **Q5 is the pivot question.** It is the only one pointing at the "Blue" category.
   Everything else builds toward it. Ed should look hardest at this one — it sits
   closest to describing a product feature.
3. **The mapping step is self-scoring, not the card's verdict** — the card never
   states what the reader has. It states what "most people" find, in general terms,
   after they've done their own mapping.
4. **No fear language, no performance claims** — checked against the brief's list
   ("before it's too late," "don't lose everything," "Blue would have protected
   you") — none appear.
5. **Open production questions, not resolved here:** the physical mail mechanics
   (address panel, postage, envelope vs. self-mailer), a CPA/attorney hand-off
   variant (their name/practice on it instead of a cold mailer), and whether a
   correction-triggered mailing is a separate variant from the always-on version.
   These are Justin's calls, flagged, not decided.
