## 4. Attacking the admission hypothesis

**What it gets right.** The barrier is probably not time. The 2021 class was four hours
and out-produced everything since. Competitors run two-night courses and one showed a
waitlist (advertising evidence; a waitlist is an organiser cap, not attendance).

**Why it fails as a thesis, five points.**

1. **It is unfalsifiable as stated.** Whether the 2021 catalog-styled piece "lowered the
   admission cost" (you attend a "learning program," not a seminar) or "borrowed trust"
   or "arrived before crowding," all three predict the same 2021 result. A hypothesis
   that absorbs the best result and the worst result by choosing which sub-variable to
   invoke has not been tested by either.
2. **Its best outside evidence cuts against "private solves it."** Bhattacharya et al.
   (*RFS* 2012, summary via search): free unbiased advice offered to about 8,000
   brokerage customers was taken up by about 5%, and those who needed it most were least
   likely to take it. That is a private, free, *trusted-sender* offer with no room and no
   audience. Take-up was still about 5%. If a warm house list refuses private free advice
   at 95%, a cold list refusing a private mirror is the base case, not a surprise.
3. **A different mechanism fits the same behaviour: information avoidance.** Karlsson,
   Loewenstein and Seppi (2009): people monitor information after good news and avoid it
   after bad news. That is a *private, self-directed* cost, no audience required. The mirror
   is a two-minute machine for finding out you may be in the wrong bucket, so it can trigger
   avoidance rather than relief. Admission and avoidance predict different things: admission
   says private wins; avoidance says private helps only if the result cannot be bad.
   The record's own FB-quiz data point ("a lot of hits," zero bookings, unclear which page
   ran) is compatible with either.
4. **Justin's own words point at a different barrier: delegation.** "They don't care how
   the steak is made" describes people who want the outcome without the mechanism. That
   is not shame. It is a preference to hand it off. The prescriptions conflict: the
   admission reading says *private, no person*; the delegation reading says *a person who
   does it for me*. Isla's candidates 1 and 2 are the two opposite prescriptions of two
   different hypotheses, presented as if they were siblings. And the mirror is itself a
   steak-making device, a two-minute self-education exercise.
5. **The outside evidence for admission as a barrier is real but small and weak.** A UK
   survey reported by Yahoo/LBC found 27% too embarrassed to ask about money (unknown
   sponsor and method, all ages; grade D). A quarter is a barrier among several, not the
   barrier.

**Verdict.** Admission is a live cost for some fraction of the population. It is not the
organising variable, and no design in the current plan discriminates it from delegation,
trust, avoidance, or "no felt problem," which is the most likely majority explanation of
all. Do not let a dead hypothesis become a hidden beam under other decisions (section 5).

## 6. The three candidates, graded

**1. Not to a room, to a person.** Right for delegators by Justin's own description, and
the only one of the three that matches his edge. But it runs straight into his own barrier
2 (advisors feel unapproachable), which is why Jayme ranked the cold one-to-one fourth
(most exposed form of that barrier; library policies bar promotion, so the gatekeeper
reappears). The way delegators get to a person is by being *sent*: by a spouse, a friend, a
CPA, an employer. Hearts & Wallets (secondary, via PlanAdviser summary, primary not
opened): friends and family were the biggest influence on increasing retirement savings
(30%), then financial professionals (22%), then life events (19%). Trust transfer is the
door. It is also the door that needs a gatekeeper (COI, employer), which Justin dislikes,
or a client (referral), which is free. **Compliance: flag to Ed and possibly Portfolio
Medics (time with an advisor).**

**2. Private and self-serve.** Grade: plausible, mostly untested, and not the same claim
as candidate 1 (section 4, point 4). Pattern-matching on "fifth route" (section 5).

**3. Event-triggered. The strongest of the three, with three qualifications.**

*Why it is strongest.* It defines the target by presence rather than absence, it works
whether or not class-ness is a filter, and it bundles *time* (fresh-start effect: Dai,
Milkman, Riis 2014, grade B for what it measured, which was diet searches, gym visits and
goal commitments; the leap to retirement consults is mine and unproven) with *not
having to declare ignorance*. Here is a mechanism I cannot verify but think is the best
idea in this document: **Justin's best conversion device is not the class, it is Step 2,
"the next step of the class you're already in, no cost."** A cold mirror card cannot
borrow that, because the reader has begun nothing. An *event* can: the packet on the kitchen
table and the deadline already running are the "already begun," and the visit is its next
step. The event turns "I don't know" into "I have a decision due." It is an inference,
not a finding (pre-flight q5).

*Supporting observations, all thin:* the one closed unit in the last drop was a pension
rollover (n=1, and a commission deal); Justin's own report that correction-time referrals
are more frequent; Hearts & Wallets 19% of new-provider trials influenced by a life event
(grade C, and it says events explain *about a fifth*, not the whole).

*Qualification 1. The two named sources fail the household test.*

- **WARN notices.** Ohio (ODJFS) and Kentucky (KY Career Center/KY Works) both publish
  public lists (confirmed by search results, pages not opened). They are *employer-level*:
  company, site, number affected, dates. There are no names, so there is no household to
  mail. A ZIP-radius mailing around a plant hits almost entirely unaffected households.
  The affected population also skews away from $250k+ IPA. And soliciting people days after
  a layoff, with a rollover on the table, is a reputational and compliance line (DOL
  rollover conflict already on record) that a "no pitch, relief" practice should not
  cross. **Kill it as a targeting source.** Its only legitimate use is as news to time
  *content*, not as a list.
- **Form 5500 terminations.** The final-return box is checked only after all assets have
  been distributed (DOL instructions, via search result). It is a *lagging* filing about a
  sponsor, not participants. Already used, correctly, as an employer-list source in
  Sloan's employer research. It is not a trigger.

*Qualification 2. What survives, in order of cost:*

1. **Self-identified intent.** People in an event search for it. Google Keyword Planner is
   free, needs only an account, and gives volume by city/county/DMA, in broad ranges
   unless there is ad spend (vendor explainers; a Google help page was in results, not
   opened). Local monthly volume for about 20 event-intent queries is a *direct, free
   measure of how big the reachable event population is in Justin's market.* Caveat I
   should state against myself: search reaches people who research at the moment of need.
   Those are class-shaped in temperament (they self-educate). It is not the delegator
   door. It is a different door into the same room.
2. **Dated milestones from birth date.** Every household on the list has one: 59.5, 62,
   the years before Medicare eligibility at 65. Universal, so it selects on *cohort*, not
   on the event actually occurring. Whether DRA can sort by birth year or month, and
   whether it sells mover, home-sale or age-in selects, is unverified: one phone call.
3. **Calendar and market timing.** Year-end statement season and the correction trigger
   already on record. January is a temporal landmark (same fresh-start caveat).
4. **Event-keyed referral ask.** A question to clients, not a line for referrers ("who do
   you know who's about to retire, or has a pension decision coming?") reaches trust
   transfer and the event together, with no gatekeeper and no spend. It extends Taleb's
   own 2026-09-12 free test (change only the referral ask for a quarter). It needs Ed's
   read and the CRM tagging that test already requires. **The record disagrees with
   itself on whether the referral ritual is live** (2026-09-16: "live from the next
   plan-delivery meeting"; 2026-09-25: "nothing built yet"). Ask Justin.
5. **Evergreen decision-aid content on the land.** A short answer to "my employer offered
   a lump sum," "I'm retiring before 65," "my date is set." Findable by the person in the
   event. Time-funded, not perk-fundable. It is the only version of the land that is not
   the steak.
6. **Justin's own plan participants.** The warmest non-class population that exists in his
   world, and the question is still open from 2026-09-19 (does he have employee-facing
   access?). It carries the DOL rollover conflict already flagged. I name it and leave it.

*Qualification 3. Geography and licence.* Justin's own point governs: there are only so
many 54-64 households inside ten miles, and event-targeting *shrinks* the count. What it
changes is the shape: a pool that **renews** (every household enters the window once) and
can be mailed **once, at the moment**, rather than the same 15,000 every spring.
Fatigue is repeated exposure to an irrelevant piece. That is the structural gain. On
licence: pension and rollover events attract Green outcomes (the closed unit was an
annuity), so any piece that describes Green stays inside OH/KY. Blue events are national
on paper, but the ten-mile draw and "office only after value shown" keep the practical
market local. Medicare and ACA-bridge events are the *cleanest* dated triggers in
retirement and are also CMS-regulated and a deferred line (ROADMAP Stage C). I flag them
and exclude them from this probe.

## 8. The probe: cheapest, asymmetric, readable

**The asymmetry.** Downside: a few hours of Justin's time, one phone call to DRA, a free
Google account, and at most roughly $1,000-$3,000 for one small mail cell. Upside: the
payoff in this practice is fat-tailed ($6M across 11 units is one or two very large
households, per the record), so cost-per-unit is a *mean* on a tail-dominated
distribution. If events are how the tail households arrive, the addressable market
changes from a stock mailed repeatedly to a renewing flow mailed at the right moment.
That is reasoned, not measured.

**Readability.** Lever 3 says a doubling cannot be read on bookings. So I do not propose
a rate comparison. Two kinds of probe are readable at this volume:

- **Censuses and conversations.** No sampling noise on a book of about 50-60 households.
- **Failures that show as a clean zero.** By the rule of three, zero stage-3 events in
  2,000 pieces puts the true rate below about 0.15% at 95%. The mirror's own bar is 0.2%
  bookings, and stage-3 events are at least as frequent as bookings. So a zero rejects
  the mirror bar for that cell, even though a "2x better" result could never be read.
  **Design the probe so that its death is readable and its success is only suggestive.**
  That is the honest shape of a small bet.

**Step 0. Free, this week, Justin's memory and one phone call.**

- 0a. **Book census by door** (class / referral / 401(k) plan / other): household count and
  AUM band. Aggregate only. No names, no balances written anywhere in memory.
- 0b. **Event at first contact?** For each door: did a live event exist (pension or
  buyout, retirement date set, job loss, inheritance, spouse's death, business sale, none)?
  Justin knows.
- 0c. **2022-2024 mail results,** and what the 2025 and 2026 pieces carried (institution
  name or not).
- 0d. **DRA call:** what event, age-in, mover or home-sale selects exist, and what they cost.
- 0e. **Keyword Planner and Google Trends:** local volume for about 20 event-intent queries.

*Kills (numbers are ⚙ guesses; Justin or Isla move them):*
- Fewer than about a third of class-door clients arrived with a live event: demote
  event-timing to a content tactic, not a lead thesis.
- Non-class-door households are not comparable in AUM to class-door: the "larger,
  wealthier delegator" story is dead. (The record already leans this way.)
- The local event-query basket sums to under about 100 searches a month: there is no
  search channel.

**Step 1. Six to eight conversations, Justin in person** (his edge; agents do not run
these). Four clients who arrived without a class; four quiet-track non-converters. Two
questions, in this order: "What was going on the week you first looked for help?" and "If
a class invitation had come in the mail, what would you have done with it?" Compliance:
a client quote used in marketing is a testimonial under the Marketing Rule. These are
research, not copy.

**Step 2. Ask, then (only if step 0 supports it) one small mail cell.**

- **2a. The event-keyed referral ask,** across one quarter's plan-delivery meetings. Free.
  Read it as names produced per meeting and the AUM band of those who book. Kill: it
  produces near-zero named people across a quarter (⚙ Justin sets the count from his own
  meeting volume).
- **2b. January cell.** One event-named variant of the mirror-then-visit piece in the
  planned drop, timed to land at year-end statement season, 54-64 pull. It rides the
  drop's print run and the drop's non-negotiable instrumentation (unique URL, QR, tracked
  phone line, source field). Marginal cost about $0.52 per piece on the 2024 quote base
  (9,000-piece run), so about $1,050 per 2,000; run alone, the test plan's short-run
  estimate of $1.00-$1.50 per piece gives $2,000-$3,000. Kill: zero stage-3 events from
  about 2,000 pieces. Success is only "suggestive" and should be read against the
  standard mirror cell's stage-3 count, not against bookings.

**What this does not test,** stated so it is not overread: whether non-class arrivals
*close.* The class produces the 90%, not the hour (Jayme's correction). A cold-booked
meeting from an event will close lower, and the cost-per-client read takes three to six
months. The mirror and any event route each need their own installation mechanism. That
was my own point 1 on 2026-09-18 and it still stands.
