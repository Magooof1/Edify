## Isla's verdict

**Agreed, and it is the strongest argument on the table:** the exposed face must carry one
idea, and the one worth carrying is the thing that makes the inside visible. That is
`ROADMAP.md`'s own 2026-09-19 trap — *differentiated on the inside, commoditized on the
outside* — and it is Justin's complaint word for word, written three weeks before he made it.

**Disagreed, and this is where he and I part:**

1. **"The record disagrees with itself" overstates it.** The ROADMAP retires paid mail as the
   *spine of Stage A*, and then on 25 September Justin says *"continue to pay the rent."* Mail
   was demoted, not abolished. The file failed to say so, which is a documentation failure and
   now fixed — not a contradiction in the plan.
2. **The 70/30 barbell is not worth January.** At ~0.26 expected bookings the aggressive leg
   observes nothing; its stated value is a prior for spring. It costs a second panel version, a
   second URL and tracked number, and an Ed round, on a drop whose four real gates have already
   slipped four times. The pile test buys more for less. **Declined, and the decline is mine.**
3. **He asked for drop results that are partly already on record.** `ROADMAP.md` lines 47–49:
   two drops of roughly $10k each produced **3 buying units and 4 attendees**, the second on a
   fresh Ohio list at a different venue — so not a saturation result. The 2023 count is still
   genuinely missing; the recent ones are not.
