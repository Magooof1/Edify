## Printed promises that need an operational counterpart before this mails

1. **"Nothing is sold in the room and there's nothing to sign."** The 2026-09-22 policy note
   (unsigned) covers the *first visit*. This piece makes the claim about the *class*. The note
   needs to say so.
2. **"The class starts from what you checked."** Operationally this needs one line in the
   class script (slide 5, attendee concerns, is the natural place): "take out your card."
   Justin's call; it is his script.
3. **"You can book [a free one-hour visit] before you leave."** True per the Step 2 Form
   ("circle your top 2," no cost, included as part of class).
4. **"Registration is required"** needs a registration tool that meets Ed's retention/export
   and state-of-residence standard.

## Measurement attached

Unique vanity URL, QR, tracked phone line, a source field on every registration. No drop goes
out without all four. Primary: **registrations per 1,000 pieces.** Instrumented stage: URL
reached. Secondary, read at the door: attendance, and the count of attendees who arrive with
the card marked. Then Step 2 visits booked, then clients (months).
