  (Justin's best guess at the larger households) may respond *less*.

**The barbell's break-even, because "probably wrong" is not an argument:**

> A letter costing c_L per piece beats the card (c_C per piece) on *expected tail per dollar*
> only if (booking uplift) x (large-household share uplift) ≥ c_L ÷ c_C.

Card marginal ≈ $0.55 a piece (⚙, see 4.0); a personalised first-class letter ≈ $4.25 ⚙
all-in. The ratio is ~8. So the letter needs, say, 3-4x the bookings (a hand-signed
personal letter against a card, practitioner lore, ungraded) *and* 2-3x the large-household
share (unmeasured). 3-4 x 2-3 = 6-12x straddles 8. **A coin flip on two multipliers nobody
has measured.** That is not a strategy to commit $10,000 to. It is a probe to run once, if
the free audit says it's worth a stamp.

**The free audit that decides the premise (Step 0 below):** compare the IPA-tier mix of the
people who came to class against the tier mix of the list they were mailed from. With ~18
units on record, only a large skew can be seen: **about a third of attendees in the top
tier against a ~15% list share (illustrative) is what ~18 units can detect at ~4%
(6 of 18).** A smaller skew is invisible at this n. I'm saying so now so a flat result
isn't read as "no gradient."

**Dismissed on the evidence, not on taste: "a few hundred very expensive touches instead
of 10,000 cheap ones."** The replacement version has no support. It also can't be read: 400
letters at even 1% response is 4 bookings.

## 2. What a power law does to every number in this file

1. **Cost per unit is the wrong decision variable for value.** It is fine for volume and
   for kills. A 3-unit drop with no large household looks like failure; one with a large
   household looks like triumph, and neither tells you the channel's rate. Mean AUM per
   unit over fewer than ~20 units is a statement about the biggest unit.
2. **So: report two numbers per channel, units and the largest household's share of the
   channel's total**, and never an average. Decisions on value need a 12-month horizon;
   decisions on volume can be made at the stage level now.
3. **Referral clients being smaller is consistent with a thinner tail from that door**, as
   well as with Taleb's homophily story. It is a count of bodies from one door, not a
   verdict.
4. **Nothing in this plan can read the tail inside 12 months.** The only tail readouts
   available are (a) Step 0's retrospective counts and (b) in 2027, the IPA tier of every
   booked meeting (tier travels on the URL and phone number, so no client data is
   needed). A distribution isn't readable until ~20 bookings.
