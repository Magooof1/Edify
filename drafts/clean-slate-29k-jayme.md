### R.3 Which dominates: payback, or the calm market? And two premises I'd word differently

**My read: they answer different questions.** Payback decides *whether mail is worth

2. **What calm removes is upside and readability.** Do not expect 2021. A December or

   failed. This tightens the standing rule in section 3.4: a calm-market miss is ambiguous

3. **Where it bites is k.** "If things aren't broken why move" predicts people who book and
   don't move money. At k = 10% the central case stops being central (R.5's corner table).
   That number lags 3-6 months and I have n=2 for it. **The risk in this plan is k, not
   price.**
4. **So the consequence is structural.** Size the calm-market commitment to what is
   *readable*, not what is *affordable*, and hold part of the money for the one condition
   under which Justin's own explanation says response should jump (U5a).

**Two premises I'd word differently.**
- **"Class frequency is dead as a lever."** Ex-2021, the two-class years (2019, 2023) average
  **$1.26M, or $0.63M per class**; the one-class years (2018, 2022, 2024) average **$0.70M**.

  one-class year beating a two-class year (2022 vs 2023, $1.075M vs $1.050M) is a rounding
  difference, and 2021 drives the rest. I'd say: *frequency is not a lever on return per

## 2. What a power law does to every number in this file

> **[REVISED 2026-10-01] Premise dead; do not quote items 2-4 as stated.** What survives is
> the *method* and not the premise: do not average over fewer than ~20 units, and say what
> a sample can and cannot read. Items 1-2 (report the largest household's share) and 4 (a
> tail readout from IPA tier) fall with the premise. Item 3 (referral clients smaller)
> rests on "size by door," which is retired at this n.

1. **Cost per unit is the wrong decision variable for value.** It is fine for volume and
   for kills. A 3-unit drop with no large household looks like failure; one with a large
   household looks like triumph, and neither tells you the channel's rate. Mean AUM per
   unit over fewer than ~20 units is a statement about the biggest unit.
2. **So: report two numbers per channel, units and the largest household's share of the
   channel's total**, and never an average. Decisions on value need a 12-month horizon;
   decisions on volume can be made at the stage level now.
3. **Referral clients being smaller is consistent with a thinner tail from that door**, as
   well as with Taleb's homophily story. It is a count of bodies from one door, not a
   verdict.
4. **Nothing in this plan can read the tail inside 12 months.** The only tail readouts
   available are (a) Step 0's retrospective counts and (b) in 2027, the IPA tier of every
   booked meeting (tier travels on the URL and phone number, so no client data is
   needed). A distribution isn't readable until ~20 bookings.
