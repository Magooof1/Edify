Asked by Justin 2026-10-09: *"If I give you a task of getting 12 units in a session. We can either do 1 day or 2.
What mailer should we use. How many should we mail. What dates should we use?"* Answered from the record only.
**Nothing here is approved.** Read `WHERE-THINGS-ARE.md` and the last section of
`drafts/offer-options-and-ranking.md` before quoting any number below.

**Assumption, stated:** "12 units" means **12 households signed up**, which is how every past drop in the record
is counted. To get 12 *attending*, divide by the show rate: 78% on Saturday in 2023 (7 of 9), 50% on Tuesday
(2 of 4), 100% in the two Ohio drops. Roughly 15 signed up for 12 in the chairs.

## Why this mailer

The only format with results in this market is the 17 x 11 class self-mailer: **1.4 to 1.7 sign-ups per 1,000 in
2020, 1.10 in 2021, 1.08 in 2023.** The one that departed from it (2025: louder, a young crowd on the cover, a red
scarcity ribbon) drew 0.40. A card is about 25 to 30% cheaper per piece (print $0.135 to $0.163 against $0.331), so it
would have to pull **at least about 70 to 75% of the self-mailer's response** to break even, and there is **no
result for a card in this market.** With one shot and a December print date, use the format with a record.

## How many: the odds of reaching 12 sign-ups, two days combined

Poisson arithmetic on three assumed response rates per 1,000 pieces. **The January rate is the thing nobody
knows**, so all three are shown.

| Pieces | Cost at $0.695 | Cost at $1.00 | If the rate is Ohio's (0.35) | **NKU 2023's (1.08)** | 2020's (1.72) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7,000 | $4,865 | $7,000 | 0% | **8%** | 54% |
| 10,000 | $6,950 | $10,000 | 0% | **40%** | 92% |
| 12,000 | $8,340 | $12,000 | 0% | **64%** | 98% |
| **14,000** | **$9,730** | **$14,000** | 0% | **82%** | 100% |
| 15,000 | $10,425 | $15,000 | 1% | 88% | 100% |
| 20,000 | $13,900 | $20,000 | 5% | 99% | 100% |

- At the 2023 rate, **50% needs about 11,000 pieces, 80% about 14,000, 90% about 15,400.**
- **$7K alone buys 7,000 to 10,000 pieces, which is 8% to 40% for 12.** It is a stretch target at $7K.
- **If the true rate is Ohio's, 12 is out of reach at any budget Justin would accept** (about 34,000 pieces). No
  mailer fixes that; January itself tells us which world we are in, and the drop cannot be adjusted after the
  December print order.

## One day or two

Saturday morning took **69% of sign-ups in 2020 and in 2023**, had the better show rate (78% against 50%), and
produced **3 of the 4 clients and $800K of the $1.05M** in 2023. Tuesday evening took **31% both times.**

**Whether Tuesday people would simply have come Saturday is not known.** If they would, one day loses nothing. If
they would not, one day loses about 30%. The two cases give very different answers:

| Pieces | 12 signed up, **two days** | 12 signed up, **Saturday only, if Tuesday adds** | 12 signed up, **Saturday only, if Tuesday is a substitute** | 12 **attending**, Saturday only (range) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | 40% | 8% | 40% | 2% to 14% |
| 14,000 | 82% | 35% | 82% | 12% to 51% |
| 20,000 | 99% | 81% | 99% | 49% to 91% |

**Two days is the choice that is safe under both readings.** If Tuesday adds, you keep it; if it only substitutes,
you lose nothing. The cost of keeping it is **about 3 hours and 15 minutes of Justin's time** (the record puts a
room at about 4.5 hours with prep) **and a thin room**: at 14,000 pieces the 2023 rate predicts about **10 sign-ups
for Saturday and about 5 for Tuesday**, of whom roughly 2 or 3 would come.

**The alternative, stated fairly:** if Justin wants **12 in one room**, one Saturday works only with **about
20,000 pieces** (about $13,900 to $20,000) for 50% to 90%. That is **$4,000 to $6,000 more** than the two-day plan
for the same odds, to save one evening.

## Dates, and one risk

- **Tuesday 16 February is the day after Presidents' Day (15 February).** Some of the audience will be away for the
  long weekend. Registration is required, so the exposure is cancellation, not a thin mailer. The 2023 Tuesday
  showed 2 of 4.
- **Retirement Planning University has been listing NKU sessions on Tuesdays at 6 pm** (2026 dates only; nothing seen
  yet for February 2027). Check its listings in January.
- NKU offered Tuesdays 2, 9 and 16 and Thursday 18; 2 and 9 are too early for the lead time, and Thursday 18 would put
  two classes in three days.

## What this does not know

- **The January response rate.** Everything above is conditional on it.
- **Unit cost.** $0.695 is Harrison's invoice; $0.83 and $1.00 are rounded totals; High Note's quote at several
  quantities replaces all of it. **Also not known: when High Note bills postage.**
- **Whether the DRA in-band count in OH/KY reaches 14,000.** If not, mail what exists and widen the radius; do not
  re-mail the same households. Added households respond less than average, so the odds above are optimistic for the
  last few thousand pieces.
- **Whether the new mailer front performs like the old one.** It changes one thing on the outside.
- The sign-up counts are small; the arithmetic is exact and the inputs are not.
