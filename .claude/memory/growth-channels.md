# Growth Channels & Marketing Budget

Lead-generation channel performance and marketing budget facts. Not client data —
business/marketing-channel knowledge only.

## Correction (2026-08-25, important — structural)
Direct mail and in-person classes are **not** two separate/independent lead-gen
channels. They are **one funnel**: class-based lead generation, filled via a mix of
(a) institutional/catalog promotion (e.g., a college's own class catalog) and (b)
targeted direct-mail invitations to attend. The mailer is a top-of-funnel tactic that
feeds the class, not a standalone channel with its own independent conversion path.
Practical consequence: the mailer's declining unit output and the classes' declining
attendance are **two measurements of the same underlying saturation**, not two
independent signals that could be softening for unrelated reasons. Do not analyze or
report on them as if they were separable channels.

## Class-based lead generation (primary channel, historical)
- Justin's primary lead-gen channel from founding through ~5 years to $22M AUM.
- Filled via two feeder tactics: institutional/catalog promotion and targeted direct-
  mail invitations (see "Direct mail as a feeder tactic" below). Both feed the same
  class attendance funnel.
- 2026-08-25: Justin flagged this channel as "starting to saturate" (his word).
- Attendance decline, confirmed (2026-08-25): over the last 2 years, some individual
  classes have drawn as few as 2 buying units (households) — a real per-class decline.
- Conversion rate is NOT the problem — confirmed (2026-08-25) it's the highest it's
  ever been: ~90% of class attendees now book a follow-up meeting. Example: a class
  last year with only 2 attendees is on track to close a $5M client.
- Saturation cause, reasonably diagnosed (2026-08-25): NOT specific to NKU or to
  Justin's own execution. Two other independent advisors Justin works alongside
  (separate businesses) report the same attendance decline. Informal research
  (friends, family, Facebook) points to format/competitor crowding — more advisors
  in the Cincinnati area have adopted the same "free college retirement class"
  format, splitting the same prospect pool across more competitors.
- Strategic implication (diagnosis only, not a decision — flagged for Taleb's
  analysis): looks like a top-of-funnel/format-crowding problem, not a demand or
  skill problem. Edify's original growth plan already names unions, credit unions,
  and employers as venue types alongside colleges, but Justin's actual channel
  history has been almost entirely college classes (NKU) — that diversification may
  be underused.

### Direct mail as a feeder tactic (not a separate channel)
- Function: invitation mechanism to drive attendance at the class-based funnel above,
  used alongside institutional/catalog promotion — not an independent lead source.
- Most recent mailer: $10,000 spent.
- Remaining 2026 direct-mail/marketing budget: $7,000.
- Historical/typical performance, confirmed (2026-08-25): historically produced
  10-20 buying units (households) per campaign, averaging ~$1.5M in AUM generated.
  This is the historical/typical range, not a per-campaign guarantee.
- **Final data on the specific $10K mailer (confirmed 2026-08-25):** produced 3
  buying units total — well below the historical 10-20 unit range (roughly a 70%+
  shortfall from even the low end).
  - Unit 1: no-go, dead lead.
  - Unit 2: pending, roughly a $1M prospect. Justin is uncertain whether this closes
    — the prospect is risk-averse. There is a real chance of no-close, not just an
    administrative delay.
  - Unit 3: closed. A pension was rolled over into an annuity, generating
    approximately $6,000 in commission revenue. Confirmed as commission revenue, not
    AUM/fee revenue — this deal type does not produce ongoing AUM the way a managed-
    account placement would, so it is not comparable to, and should not be used to
    estimate, an AUM figure.
- **ROI status: unresolved, binary/high-variance — not a success or failure call.**
  This mailer's real return currently hinges almost entirely on whether the ~$1M
  pending prospect (Unit 2) closes. If it closes, the mailer produced one real AUM
  relationship plus a commission deal; if it doesn't, the $10K produced only ~$6K in
  one-time commission revenue against a $1.5M historical AUM baseline. Record the
  range, don't collapse it to a verdict.
